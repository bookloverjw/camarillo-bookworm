# Supabase auth email subjects

Paste each into the matching template's Subject field.

- **confirm-signup.html** → `Confirm your email for The Bookworm`
- **reset-password.html** → `Reset your password for The Bookworm`
- **magic-link.html** → `Your sign-in link for The Bookworm`
- **change-email.html** → `Confirm your new email address`
- **invite-user.html** → `You're invited to The Bookworm`
- **reauthentication.html** → `{{ .Token }} is your Bookworm verification code`
